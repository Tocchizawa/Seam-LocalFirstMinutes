## Assumptions

- A native disclosure is the intended compact interaction: it starts collapsed, shows the current project’s recovered-note count, and retains the existing per-session open action when expanded.
- Switching projects should reset the disclosure to its collapsed state.
- A 10rem internal scroll area is sufficient to bound the expanded list while keeping every recovered note reachable.

## Deviations

- None. No recovery records or session data were deleted or altered.
